Frequently asked questions

How is a child's UK school year worked out from their birthday?

In England, the school year a child starts is generally determined by whether their fourth birthday falls before the start of the September term — children born within a single academic year (1 September to 31 August) are normally in the same school year, though local authority rules can vary slightly.
