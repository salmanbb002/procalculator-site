Frequently asked questions

Why does the UK use both metric and imperial units?

The UK officially adopted the metric system decades ago for most contexts (science, most retail), but imperial units (miles, pints, feet and inches for height) remain in everyday and legal use in specific areas — road distances and speed limits are a well-known example.
