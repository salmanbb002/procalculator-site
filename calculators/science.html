🔬 Science Calculators UK

Science and unit conversion calculators for converting between measurement systems quickly and accurately — essential for coursework, cooking, DIY and everyday cross-unit conversions.

The UK famously mixes metric and imperial units in everyday life — recipes in grams, human height in feet and inches, fuel efficiency in miles per gallon — which makes reliable unit conversion a genuinely useful everyday tool, not just a science-class exercise. Getting a conversion factor slightly wrong compounds fast in anything from a recipe to a construction measurement.

This category is built to be a fast, accurate reference for the length, mass, volume and other unit conversions that come up constantly, whichever direction you need to go.

Beyond conversions, several tools here cover core physics and chemistry formulas — wave frequency, escape velocity, moment of force, dilution using C1V1=C2V2 — using SI units alongside the everyday units UK students and hobbyists actually encounter, so the working is easy to check against a textbook or exam board formula sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the UK use both metric and imperial units?

The UK officially adopted the metric system decades ago for most contexts (science, most retail), but imperial units (miles, pints, feet and inches for height) remain in everyday and legal use in specific areas — road distances and speed limits are a well-known example.
