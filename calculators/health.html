❤️ Health & Fitness Calculators UK

Health and fitness calculators for BMI, calories and everyday wellbeing estimates — quick, general-guidance tools, not a substitute for medical advice.

BMI, calorie needs and due dates are some of the most-searched health calculations, and also some of the most misunderstood — BMI in particular is a simple screening ratio that doesn't distinguish muscle from fat, so it's a starting point for a conversation, not a diagnosis. Calorie and BMR (basal metabolic rate) estimates use established formulas based on height, weight, age and activity level, but individual metabolism varies, and a due date calculation is an estimate based on average pregnancy length, not a guaranteed date.

These calculators are built to give a fast, well-explained estimate using standard, recognised formulas — useful for general awareness, but always a starting point rather than a medical result.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI an accurate measure of health for everyone?

BMI is a useful general screening tool but doesn't account for muscle mass, frame size, age or ethnicity, so it can be misleading for athletes, older adults or anyone with an atypical body composition. It's best used alongside other measures, not in isolation.

How accurate is a pregnancy due date calculator?

Due date calculators estimate based on average pregnancy length (typically 40 weeks from the last menstrual period), but only a small percentage of babies actually arrive on the calculated date — it's a useful planning estimate, not a fixed deadline. Your midwife or doctor's dating scan is the more accurate reference.
