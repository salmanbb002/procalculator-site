Childcare Cost Estimator

Estimate monthly nursery or childcare costs.

How it works

This multiplies your childcare provider's hourly rate by hours per day and days per week to give a weekly cost, then projects that to monthly and annual figures — since real childcare costs vary enormously by provider, region and type of care, you enter your own rate rather than the tool assuming a national average.

UK context

UK families may be eligible for government support that significantly reduces the real cost of childcare — this includes free hours schemes for eligible children, Tax-Free Childcare (where the government tops up money you pay into a childcare account), and the childcare element of Universal Credit for eligible working families. None of these are automatically applied here since eligibility depends on individual circumstances.

Tips

  • Check gov.uk's childcare calculator and eligibility checker to see which support schemes you might qualify for — the potential savings can be substantial and are easy to miss if you're not actively checking.
  • Rates and availability of free hours can vary by local authority and by your child's age — a 2-year-old, 3-year-old and school-age child can all have different entitlements.
  • Some providers offer discounts for multiple children or block-booking — worth asking about directly rather than assuming the standard hourly rate always applies.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need to enter my own hourly rate?

Childcare costs vary hugely by region, provider type (nursery, childminder, nanny) and local market conditions — there's no single accurate UK average, so using your own quoted rate gives a far more realistic result.

Does this account for free childcare hours or Tax-Free Childcare?

No — this calculates the raw cost based on the rate and hours you enter. Check gov.uk's childcare support eligibility checker separately, since these schemes can significantly reduce your actual out-of-pocket cost if you qualify.

A quick note

Uses the rate and hours you enter, since actual costs vary hugely by region and provider. Doesn't automatically account for government support schemes — check gov.uk for what you may be eligible for.
