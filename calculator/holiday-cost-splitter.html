Holiday Cost Splitter

Split total trip costs fairly between travellers.

What is the Holiday Cost Splitter?

A holiday cost splitter works out how much each traveller owes when a group trip's total cost needs to be shared evenly, with an option to account for costs one person has already covered. It works by dividing the total trip cost by the number of travellers to get an equal per-person share, then subtracting anything a specific person has already prepaid from what they still owe the group. To calculate it manually: total cost ÷ number of people = cost per person; if one person already paid for something on everyone's behalf, subtract their share of that from what they owe overall. This tool handles a simple even split — for itemised splitting where people paid different amounts for different things, you'll need to track individual contributions separately.

How it works

This divides the total trip cost evenly across the number of travellers, then accounts for any amount one person has already prepaid on behalf of the group to show what everyone else still owes them.

UK context

An even split works well when everyone shares costs equally, but group trips often involve some people paying for specific things upfront (accommodation booked on one card, activities paid by another person) — tracking who's already covered what is where cost splitting gets genuinely complicated, which is exactly the gap this simple prepaid-amount field is meant to help with.

Tips

  • For trips with lots of individually itemised costs (some people paying for different things), a dedicated shared expense-tracking app is usually more practical than manual splitting, since it can track every individual contribution rather than just one lump prepaid amount.
  • Decide upfront whether costs are being split evenly regardless of individual spending (e.g. on food/drinks) or itemised individually — agreeing the approach before the trip avoids awkward conversations afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Does this handle people paying for different individual things?

Only in a simple way — it accounts for one prepaid amount by one person, but for multiple people paying for different individual items, a dedicated expense-tracking app that logs each contribution separately is more practical.

How do I split costs that some people don't want to share equally?

This calculator assumes an even split across all travellers — for uneven splits (e.g. someone not participating in a specific paid activity), you'll need to adjust the total or calculate that portion separately.
