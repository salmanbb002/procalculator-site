Netflix Binge Time Calculator

Work out how long it would take to binge your favourite show.

What is the Netflix Binge Time Calculator?

A Netflix binge time calculator works out the total time it would take to watch an entire TV show, and how many days that represents at your own typical viewing pace. It works by multiplying the number of episodes by the average episode length in minutes to get total watch time, converting that to hours, then dividing by how many hours a day you plan to watch to estimate the number of days to finish. To calculate it manually: episodes × average episode length (minutes) = total minutes, divide by 60 for hours, then divide by your hours-per-day viewing pace for the number of days — a handy way to plan a binge-watch or just see how much of your life a show represents.

How it works

This multiplies episode count by average episode length to get total watch time, then divides by your planned daily watching hours to estimate how many days a full binge would take — works for any show, on any streaming service, using whatever numbers you enter.

UK context

Binge-watching an entire series back to back is a genuinely popular way to consume TV now, and seeing the total time commitment laid out plainly — sometimes running to multiple full days of screen time for a long-running show — can be a fun (or slightly alarming) reality check before diving in.

Tips

  • Episode lengths often vary within a season (a finale is frequently longer than a regular episode) — using an average is a reasonable simplification for a quick estimate.
  • Be realistic about your planned daily watching hours for the most useful "days to finish" estimate — most people don't sustain their opening-weekend binge pace for an entire series.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for any show, not just Netflix?

Yes — it's a general binge-time calculator that works with the episode count and length you enter, applicable to any streaming service or show.

Does this account for skipping intros or credits?

No — it uses the average episode length you enter as-is. If you typically skip intros, you could enter a slightly shorter average length to reflect your actual viewing pattern.
