Term-Time Only Salary Calculator

Convert a full-year salary to a term-time-only equivalent.

How it works

This pro-rates a full-year-equivalent salary by the proportion of the year you're actually paid for — your weeks worked (typically around 38-39 weeks for a standard UK school year) plus your statutory holiday entitlement weeks, out of the 52.14 weeks in a full year.

UK context

Term-time-only roles (common for teaching assistants, school support staff and similar positions) are usually quoted as a full-year-equivalent salary — the figure you'd earn if the role were full-year — with actual pay pro-rated down to reflect the weeks actually worked plus paid holiday entitlement, which is why the take-home figure looks quite different from the headline salary advertised.

Tips

  • Some employers spread term-time-only pay evenly across all 12 months (so you receive the same amount each month, including school holidays) rather than paying only during term weeks — check your specific contract, since this changes your monthly cash flow even though the annual total is the same.
  • Statutory minimum holiday entitlement is prorated for part-year workers too — check that your holiday pay calculation reflects your actual pattern correctly.

Frequently asked questions

How is a term-time-only salary calculated from the full-year figure?

The full-year-equivalent salary is multiplied by the proportion of paid weeks (weeks worked plus holiday entitlement) out of the full 52.14 weeks in a year — this pro-rates the salary down to reflect actual weeks paid.

Will I be paid only during term time, or spread across the year?

This depends on your employer's specific payroll practice — some pay only in worked/holiday weeks (larger paydays, unpaid gaps), while others spread the same annual total evenly across all 12 months. Check your contract.

A quick note

Pro-rates by paid weeks (worked + statutory holiday entitlement) out of 52.14 weeks in a year. Check your specific contract for how pay is actually spread across the year.
