Wavelength & Frequency Calculator

Convert between wavelength and frequency for any wave.

What is the Wavelength & Frequency Calculator?

A wavelength and frequency calculator converts between a wave's wavelength and its frequency, using the fundamental relationship that wave speed equals frequency multiplied by wavelength (v = fλ). It works by rearranging that formula depending on which value you know: if you enter frequency, wavelength is calculated as speed ÷ frequency; if you enter wavelength, frequency is calculated as speed ÷ wavelength. For light and other electromagnetic waves, the speed used is the speed of light in a vacuum (299,792,458 m/s); for sound, wave speed varies by medium and temperature, with 343 m/s being a typical value for air at room temperature. To calculate it manually, use λ = v ÷ f or f = v ÷ λ with the appropriate wave speed for your situation.

How it works

Every wave follows the same fundamental relationship: wave speed equals frequency multiplied by wavelength. Rearranging this formula lets you calculate wavelength from frequency (or vice versa) provided you know the wave's speed — for light and other electromagnetic waves travelling through a vacuum, that speed is the universal constant known as the speed of light.

UK context

Sound waves and light waves behave very differently here: light's speed in a vacuum is a fixed universal constant, while sound's speed depends entirely on the medium it's travelling through (and, for a given medium like air, on temperature) — there's no single "speed of sound" the way there's a fixed speed of light, which is why this calculator lets you specify a wave speed for sound.

Tips

  • For light and other electromagnetic waves, always use the vacuum speed of light unless you're specifically accounting for the wave slowing down in a different medium (like light travelling through glass or water).
  • Sound speed increases with temperature in air — 343 m/s is a commonly used value for dry air at roughly room temperature, but it changes with conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the relationship between wavelength and frequency?

Wave speed = frequency × wavelength. For a fixed wave speed, wavelength and frequency are inversely related — higher frequency means shorter wavelength, and vice versa.

Does the speed of sound change?

Yes, significantly — unlike the speed of light in a vacuum (a fixed constant), the speed of sound depends on the medium it travels through and, within a given medium like air, on temperature and other conditions.

A quick note

Light/EM waves use the speed of light in a vacuum (a fixed constant); sound speed varies by medium and temperature, so the value used is illustrative unless you enter your own measured figure.
