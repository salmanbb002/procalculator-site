Fencing Cost Calculator

Estimate panels, posts and cost for a garden fence.

How it works

The number of fence panels needed is your total fence length divided by the width of a single panel, rounded up — you can't buy a fraction of a panel. The number of posts is always one more than the number of panels, since each panel needs a post on both sides, and adjacent panels share a post.

UK context

Standard UK fence panels are commonly sold in a width of around 1.83m (6 feet), though sizes vary by manufacturer and style, so it's worth checking the specific panel you're buying rather than assuming the standard width.

Tips

  • This calculation excludes concrete (or postcrete) for setting posts, gravel boards, fixings and labour — factor these in separately for a complete budget.
  • Check ground conditions before finalising your post plan — very hard, rocky or waterlogged ground can significantly affect post-setting time and cost.
  • If your fence run isn't a neat multiple of your panel width, you may need a cut-down panel at one end — budget for a little extra material to allow for this.

Frequently asked questions

How many posts do I need for a fence?

One more than the number of panels — each panel needs a post at each end, and neighbouring panels share the post between them, so posts = panels + 1.

Does this calculator include concrete for setting posts?

No — it covers panel and post material cost only. Concrete/postcrete, gravel boards, fixings and labour should be budgeted separately.

A quick note

Uses the unit prices you enter and excludes concrete, gravel boards, fixings and labour. Check current prices with your local merchant for an accurate total.
